title: Collections

A collection is an album, a book, a season: works kept together **in an
order**, with a goal - how many it is meant to hold, and by when - and a
verdict worked out from the works inside. What a collection can be is your
profile's word for it
([`collection_kinds`](/kilna/reference/profile-document/#work_kinds-release_kinds-collection_kinds)):
the Studio profile makes albums, singles and cycles, a novel books and parts.

A collection is **one level deep**. It holds works, never other collections,
and a work is in one collection at most - putting it in another takes it out
of the first.

## The screen

**Collections** is the first entry under *Library* in the sidebar. Each
collection is a card:

- **its kind and how full it is** - *7 of 12*, green once the target is met,
  amber until then, just a count when it has no target;
- **its name**, and under it the bar to the target;
- **the goal in words** - *Due Dec 1 · 5 to go*. A day gone by with works
  still to come is said in red; a collection that met its target is simply
  *full*;
- **the verdict** - the average of the scores of what it holds, and **the
  weakest link**: the lowest-scored work, the track that makes someone skip.
  A work nobody has scored counts towards nothing - it is a question, not a
  zero - and the card says how many there are.

The dashed card at the end, and **New collection** at the top, make one: a
name and a kind. The goal and the order are set on the collection itself.

## An open collection

Click a card to open it beside the grid; the grid narrows to a column so the
others stay a click away. The open collection is part of the address, so
**Back** walks between collections.

At the top, the name - click it to rename - and the kind. Under it:

- **Goal** - *Meant to hold* (a count) and *Done by* (a day), each changed in
  place; the bar and the sentence under them follow.
- **By the scores** - the average, of how many scored works, and the weakest
  and the strongest work by name, a click from their cards.
- **Description** - a line on what holds it together.
- **Works, in order** - the running order. Drag a row by its grip to move
  it, or focus a row and press **Alt + ↑** / **Alt + ↓**. The **×** at the end
  of a row takes the work out; the work itself stays in the catalogue.

Every change to the order, an addition or a removal is **one step back**:
the toast offers to undo it, and **Ctrl+Z** does the same. Undoing puts every
work back exactly where it stood - a work taken from another album goes back
to that album, at its old place.

At the foot, **Add a work** finds one by name, and **Show in the catalogue**
opens the catalogue narrowed to this collection. **Delete collection**, in the
menu at the top, sends it to the [trash](/kilna/guides/the-trash/); its works
stay where they are, in no collection, and come back into it if you restore
it.

## Filling a collection

The works of an album are found in the catalogue, so that is where most of
them go in from:

- **Tick and send.** Tick the works, then **To collection** in the bar at
  the foot, and pick the collection - or **New collection…**, which makes one
  with them already inside. They go in **after its last work**, in the order
  the table shows them. A work that is already there is passed over rather
  than moved to the end.
- **Carry rows onto it.** Press on a row and move it: while it is in the air
  a shelf of your collections stands at the foot of the catalogue, and the
  row goes into the one you let go over. Picking up a ticked row carries
  every ticked row with it. While the catalogue is grouped by collection, a
  collection's heading takes the rows as well. **Escape** puts them back.
- **From the work.** The chip after the verdict in a work's header names its
  collection - dashed *No collection* when it is in none. Click it to put
  the work in another, make a new one for it, open the one it is in, or take
  it out.

## Finding a collection's works

- `collection:` in the catalogue's box narrows to one -
  `collection:"Deep time"`. The name is what you type and what the box shows;
  see [narrowing from the box](/kilna/guides/the-catalogue/#narrowing-from-the-box).
- **Group by collection** folds the catalogue into a block per collection,
  with the works in none at the bottom.
